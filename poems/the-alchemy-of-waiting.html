The Alchemy of Waiting

A poem by himquantum
In the space between the want and the arrival, something ancient stirs. Not yet, but almost— this is where the real work happens. The caterpillar waits patiently in its cocoon, dissolving into metamorphosis. The old form must die before the wings learn to beat. There is a strange alchemy in longing— it hollows you out and fills you simultaneously. Your hands learn the shape of what you cannot yet hold. Your heart recognizes the frequency of a thing that hasn't arrived. Every day you do not have what you want, you are being refined. The pressure of absence carves you into something new. The person who wanted it naively is already gone. I have seen people arrive at their desires only to find themselves strangers to the gift. They did not wait long enough. They skipped the metamorphosis. But those who pursue with patience— who know the happiness of pursuit itself, who do not confuse the destination with the transformation— they arrive different. Worthy. Changed so fundamentally that what they wanted is no longer what they need. So I do not rush this space between. I do not wish the waiting away. The alchemy is not want becoming having. The alchemy is want becoming wisdom. And by the time what I sought finally arrives, I will have already transformed into someone worthy of receiving it. Or someone wise enough to recognize it was never what I needed all along. Either way, the furnace has done its work.
