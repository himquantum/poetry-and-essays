Law & Morality

A poem by himquantum
If morality shapes law, then whose hand writes the code of right? Whose whims win? The saint's prayer, the tyrant's will, or the crowd that changes with the night? Fluid morality cant dictate law, for law can chain the body while the spirit still wanders proud. Morality begins when justification collapses, when the hand that struck finally recognizes it has struck itself. So the guilty are not merely punished. They are haunted until suffering teaches what mercy could have taught sooner. And the cruelest prison has never been built of stone. It is the mind that has seen the truth and can no longer hide inside its beautiful lies.
