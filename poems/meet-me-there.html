Meet me there

A poem by himquantum
Not here, where words are made for weather and for small talks where we ask how are you and never mean it and answer fine — Means nothing Meet me there - past the small talk and the careful smiles, past the version of myself I keep presentable for society Past the noise we call conversation and the silence we mistake for peace. There is a place I go when the night runs long and the questions grow teeth. Where thoughts move freely, where nothing is too strange to say, where we can hold an idea up to the light and admit together we do not know what it is. Meet me there. Bring your doubts. Bring the book that changed you and the one that broke you open. Bring your unfinished arguments with God, with time, with yourself. I do not need you to have answers. I only need you to have questions and the nerve to ask them out loud, without apology, in the dark, past midnight, past comfort. I have been waiting not for someone to agree with me but for someone to push back hard enough that the sparks are worth watching. Meet me there - that world without borders where the mind runs barefoot and everything is permitted except pretending. I will be the one Waiting for you.
