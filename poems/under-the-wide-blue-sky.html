Under the Wide Blue Sky

A poem by himquantum
There is a quiet that arrives when the morning stretches wide, and the sky, that patient blue, wears its clouds like scattered thoughts that never quite decide to land. I have learned to live beneath it, not chasing, not holding on, just breathing where the light falls soft through the gaps between the white. The grass keeps its slow counsel. The wind moves through the pines like a hand smoothing a bedsheet. Nothing here asks for more than the moment it is given. Some days the clouds gather into shapes I almost recognize, a face, a bird, a distant shore, and then they drift apart again, teaching me that even beauty knows when to let go. I am not the sky. I am not the cloud. I am the small warm thing standing in the field below, feeling the sun on my shoulders and calling it enough. That is the whole of it, really. The sky does not hurry. Neither should I.
