O Autumn

A poem by himquantum
In autumn's tender, humble fall, A quiet grandeur cloaks it all. Leaves in gold and crimson blaze, Awakening hearts in a fleeting haze. No sorrow in fall, they drift, they lift, Their parting hues, a beautiful gift. Each leaf descends, a soft embrace, Adorning earth with fleeting grace. They whisper life's unending spins That endings gleam where dawn begins In sacrifice, they pave the way For spring's rebirth, for hope's new day. O autumn, bold in modest flight, You bathe the world in warmth and light. Your fall, a triumph, soft and grand, Sows seeds of life across the land.
